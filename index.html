Word Roots - Privacy Policy

Developer: Brainy Elephant

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. About Word Roots

Word Roots is an educational word game developed by Brainy Elephant. This policy explains how information may be processed when you use the application.

2. Game Progress

Word Roots saves game progress locally on your device. This information is used to remember your progress through the game.

3. Advertising

Word Roots integrates Google AdMob to display advertising. Google's advertising services may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions and diagnostic information, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

Google's privacy policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Information Sharing

Information may be processed by Google in connection with advertising services. Brainy Elephant does not sell players' locally stored game progress.

5. Data Retention

Game progress is stored on the device and can generally be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application. Data processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable retention practices.

6. Children's Privacy

Word Roots is intended for a general educational audience and is not specifically directed at children. Advertising and privacy settings will be configured in accordance with applicable requirements.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's features, advertising arrangements or data practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact Brainy Elephant at brainyelephant1001@gmail.com.
